Privacy Policy

STL/3MF to STEP Converter — stltostep.app

Last updated: August 19, 2026

The short version

This tool converts 3D model files entirely inside your own browser. Your STL, 3MF, or ZIP file is never uploaded to any server — the conversion happens locally on your device, and the resulting STEP file never leaves your computer unless you choose to save or share it yourself. We do not collect, see, or store the files you convert.

Who is responsible for this site

This site is an independent, non-commercial project run by an individual developer. If you have any question about this policy or how your data is handled, you can write to privacy@stltostep.app.

Your files

All file parsing, smoothing, and STEP conversion runs as JavaScript and WebAssembly code inside your browser tab. No file you drag, select, or convert is ever sent over the network to us or to any third party. If you close the tab, the file and its converted result are gone — nothing is retained on our side because nothing ever reaches our side.

This site does not currently set any cookies and does not run any analytics or tracking script. It does not ask you to create an account and does not store any personal data about you.
